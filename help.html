Account & Profile

How do I verify my identity (KYC)?
Go to Menu > Account Settings > Verify Identity. You will need to upload a clear photo of your National ID, Passport, or Driver's License (front and back), along with a live selfie. Verification usually takes up to 24 hours.
Can I change my registered email address?
For security reasons, email addresses cannot be changed directly from the app. Please contact our Live Support team with your account details, and they will assist you with the update.

Deposits & Withdrawals

What payment methods are supported?
We currently support M-Pesa (instant mobile money), USDT (TRC20 network), and major Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard).
How long do withdrawals take?
M-Pesa withdrawals are typically processed within 1-2 hours. USDT (TRC20) withdrawals depend on blockchain network congestion but usually take 10-30 minutes. Card withdrawals may take 1-3 business days.

Trading & Indices

What are Synthetic Indices?
Synthetic indices simulate real-world market volatility but are generated by a cryptographically secure random number generator. They are available 24/7, unaffected by real-world market closures or news events.
How does the "Refer & Earn" commission work?
You earn 10% of your referral's net trading losses daily. Commissions are calculated at midnight and added to your pending balance. Once your pending balance reaches $1.00, it becomes available for withdrawal.

Security

How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Go to Menu > Account Settings > Two-Factor Auth (2FA). Download an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator), scan the QR code provided, and enter the 6-digit code to activate. This is highly recommended to protect your funds.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address, and we will send you a secure link to reset your password. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within 5 minutes.
